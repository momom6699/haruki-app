A running journal

Write down the day you ran.

Haruki keeps a run and that day's entry together in one place. Your records can stay on your device.

How it works

From before the run to reading it back.

Run

One tap. The screen can stay off.

Flip the switch on the Today screen. You can put the phone away and let the screen go dark — recording carries on.

It stays

Distance, time and route, already written.

When you finish, the run becomes a page in the journal. A title and the entry itself can be added later.

So do the songs

What you heard, kept with the run.

The title and artist of whatever was playing during the run are kept with it, so you can find that day’s music again.

It adds up

The days you ran fill in the month.

A day’s shade comes from how far you went. How many runs and how many kilometres that month sit quietly above.

Read it back

One page a day, like a book.

Search by date, or by the words you wrote. The weather and the songs from that day are still attached.

How your data is handled

Runs, entries, and photos are stored on your device. Haruki does not send them to a server of its own.

Backing up to Google Drive is optional. Haruki only touches the file it created there and never the other files in your Drive. Health Connect is read-only — Haruki never writes back to it. プライバシーポリシー（日本語）
