About the app

Haruki is a journaling app that lets you record daily events and your physical condition with ease. Your journal data can be backed up to your own Google Drive, so you can safely carry it over when switching devices. Haruki can also connect with Health Connect to bring health data such as step count and sleep into your journal entries.
