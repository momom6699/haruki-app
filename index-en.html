A running journal

Write down the day you ran.

Haruki keeps a run and that day's entry together in one place. Your records can stay on your device.

What it does

Records the run

It records your route and your time. While you are running, the only number on screen is elapsed time.

Holds the day's entry

Add a note, photos, and the song you had on. You can write on the days you did not run, too.

Lets it accumulate

The days you ran stay on the calendar and the heatmap. The darker a day sits, the further you went.

14 August 2026 · 5.2 km Took a long time to get out the door. The rain stopped just before the bridge, and after that it was easy.

How your data is handled

Runs, entries, and photos are stored on your device. Haruki does not send them to a server of its own.

Backing up to Google Drive is optional. Haruki only touches the file it created there and never the other files in your Drive. Health Connect is read-only — Haruki never writes back to it. プライバシーポリシー（日本語）
