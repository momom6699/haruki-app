Journaling app

Every day you keep,
grows into a ring.

Haruki is a journaling app for writing down your day, or how you felt, in just a few lines. Entries build up quietly in your own Google Drive, and if you connect Health Connect, step count and sleep come along too.

Read the Privacy Policy

What it does

A few lines a day

Jot down what happened, or how you felt, whenever it comes to mind. A plain entry screen that stays out of the way of the habit.

Backs up quietly to Google Drive

Haruki uses only the drive.file scope, so it can only reach the backup file it created itself — nothing else in your Drive. Switch phones, and your journal comes with you.

Loosely connects with Health Connect

Step count and sleep duration come in read-only, alongside your entries. Haruki never holds write access to Health Connect.
